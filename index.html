Arcane Roll Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Arcane Roll is a dice roller for Root, made by OddBitty. You can roll in chat with /roll, or at the 3D table in the app. This page explains what information the app reads and keeps, why, and who can see it.

What the app reads

To spot a /roll, the app reads each new message in the channels it can see. It only acts on messages that start with /roll. It does not save any other messages. Community leaders choose which channels the app can see.

What the app keeps

  • Latest rolls: the last 50 rolls for each game. A game is a channel your community's leaders have picked. Each one has your Root nickname, the dice you rolled, the results and any bonus. Older rolls drop off as new ones come in.
  • Your dice, table and game: the dice set, table and game you picked, saved with your Root user ID so they are ready next time.
  • Game channels: which channels your community's leaders picked as games.

What the app does not keep

  • The app does not save your profile picture or any other details from your Root profile, apart from your nickname on your rolls.
  • The app does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • The app does not send your information to any service outside Root.

Where your information is stored

Your rolls and your choices are stored in the storage that Root provides for the app. Each community where the app is installed has its own rolls. Root's own privacy policy covers how Root handles your account and data.

Your device also remembers where you put the Latest rolls card, how big you made it, and whether the table moves. This stays on your device.

Who can see it

  • When you roll, the app posts the result with your nickname. A /roll is answered in the same channel. A roll at the table is posted in the channel of the game you are playing.
  • Anyone using the app in your community can see the games and their latest rolls with nicknames, including a game in a private channel. A /roll in a channel that is not a game stays in that channel.

Removing your information

Your rolls leave the latest rolls list as new ones come in. Messages the app has posted in chat stay in that channel, like any other message. If you would like to know what the app holds about you, or ask us to remove it, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it keeps, we will update this page and the date at the top.
